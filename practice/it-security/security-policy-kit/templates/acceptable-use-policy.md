## 1. Purpose

Protect Example Corp's people, customers, and data while letting everyone work productively.

## 2. You may

- Use company devices and accounts for work, and for **limited, reasonable personal use** (e.g. checking
  personal email at lunch) that doesn't break the rules below.
- Install software from the **Company Portal / approved list**. Ask the service desk for anything else.

## 3. You must

- **Lock your screen** when you step away (`Win + L` / `Ctrl + Cmd + Q`).
- Keep your password to yourself and use the company **password manager**; use **MFA** when asked.
- Store work files in **approved locations** (OneDrive / SharePoint / file shares), not on personal
  cloud storage or USB drives.
- **Report** lost devices, suspicious emails ("Report phishing" button), and suspected incidents to the
  service desk **immediately** - you won't be in trouble for reporting.
- Allow updates and restarts to install.

## 4. You must not

- Share accounts, or use someone else's.
- Disable or bypass security tools (antivirus, firewall, VPN, web filtering).
- Install unapproved software, remote-access tools, or browser extensions.
- Access, download, or share illegal, offensive, or pirated content.
- Connect personal devices to the internal network (use the **guest Wi-Fi**).
- Send confidential data (see [Data Classification](data-classification.md)) to personal accounts.

## 5. Monitoring and privacy

Company systems are monitored for security and operational purposes, in line with law and our
privacy notice. [Describe what is logged, e.g. sign-ins, web filtering, email security scanning.]

## 6. Consequences

Breaking this policy may lead to access being removed and to disciplinary action, in line with HR
policy. Honest mistakes reported promptly are treated as learning opportunities.
